# Industrial Twin Lab 2026 design refresh

## Intent

Preserve the approved Research Monograph identity while making the August 2026 field update legible as a system change, not a decorative redesign. The public surface remains English, static-first, visibly experimental, and evidence-led.

## Visual system

- Dark graphite shell and warm paper remain the primary surface pair.
- Serif display type carries theses and section claims; monospace type carries labels, dates, identifiers, controls, and provenance.
- Signal orange remains sparse and semantic: safety stop, boundary, traceability, or active evidence state.
- Thin rules continue to express structure. Cards, gradients, and application-dashboard chrome remain out of scope.
- Large statements use editorial scale; technical diagrams use dense but readable ledgers.

## 2026 system model

The refreshed hero and architecture atlas use four layers: Data, Context, Decision and process orchestration, and Actuation. A digital-thread label connects the model. Phase 1 stops before Actuation, and the stop is shown in signal orange at desktop and mobile widths.

## Decision replay

The demonstrator presents a P-101 decision replay instead of elevating the experiment identifier as the main result. Context snapshot, constraint check, evidence state, and authority form the audit rail. The deterministic fixture, synthetic disclosure, and human-review requirement remain unchanged.

## Responsive behavior

At narrow widths, four-column system diagrams become one ordered vertical ledger. The actuation boundary changes from a left stop rule to a top stop rule so the reading direction remains unambiguous. Source-ledger links become one-column rows with full touch targets.
