# Glossary

The glossary fixes a shared vocabulary for the publication. Definitions are deliberately narrower than marketing usage: each term must be useful when specifying an experiment, reviewing evidence, or assigning authority.

The distinctions matter. **Verification** asks whether an implementation conforms to its design; **validation** asks whether the result is fit for the intended engineering decision. A **Champion Model** is selected only under stated evidence and constraints, while a **Challenger Model** remains comparable under the same conditions. Neither title grants operational authority.
