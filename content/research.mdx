# Open Research Questions

Industrial Twin Lab publishes uncertainty instead of hiding it behind a product narrative. The catalogue is deliberately bounded to fifteen questions. Each question can accumulate hypotheses, experiments, negative results, replications, and revisions without being mistaken for a solved claim.

The 2026 frontier adds five questions about governed agent systems, reproducible context, simulation-system packaging, connected-product data contracts, and evidence for high-risk industrial AI. They respond to the <a href="https://www.digitaltwinconsortium.org/press-room/digital-twin-consortium-announces-the-digital-twin-system-framework/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Digital Twin System Framework</a>, the <a href="https://www.digitaltwinconsortium.org/2026/02/the-industrial-ai-agent-manifesto-governance-requirements-for-trustworthy-autonomous-operations/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Industrial AI Agent Manifesto</a>, <a href="https://modelica.org/news/2025-01-08-ssp-20-released/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">SSP 2.0</a>, and European data and AI obligations without treating any framework as a solved engineering method.

The 4 September 2026 review refreshes two evidence dependencies. <a href="https://modelica.org/news/release-modelica-specification-3.7/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Modelica 3.7</a> is now the current language specification. <a href="https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/1744/oj" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Regulation (EU) 2026/1744</a> amended the AI Act schedule: the relevant high-risk provisions apply from 2 December 2027 for Annex III systems and 2 August 2028 for Annex I product systems. These dates are versioned research context, not legal advice or evidence that an industrial system is compliant.

Maturity is not a software purchase. P-101 does not advance because a model was trained; it advances only when evidence supports a defined engineering decision across relevant regimes. A higher level also inherits every lower-level obligation: data quality, traceability, simulation validity, uncertainty, and human authority.
