# About Industrial Twin Lab

**Status: Research / Experimental**

Industrial Twin Lab is a living technical manifesto, research publication, architecture atlas, and interactive concept demonstrator for industrial machine intelligence. It begins with a strict proposition: an AI system should perform its first experiment in a validated digital environment, not on an operational machine. The publication is authored and maintained in the public <a href="https://github.com/aserdargun/itl-aserdargun-com" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">aserdargun/itl-aserdargun-com source repository</a>.

It is not a commercial SaaS workflow, a control system, a safety system, a production-data service, or a claim that the P-101 fixture describes a real plant. Phase 1 has no operational-technology connection, backend API, account system, model-training runtime, or automated deployment authority. P-101 and all displayed experiment values are fictional, deterministic teaching fixtures.

The August 2026 editorial refresh aligns the research map with current digital-twin-system, industrial-agent, simulation-interoperability, data-access, and AI-governance developments. The Phase 1 runtime and authority boundary remains unchanged: static publication, deterministic local fixture, no backend, and no control path.

## Roadmap

1. **Phase 1 — Manifesto + Architecture Atlas:** establish the thesis, safety boundary, typed knowledge layer, P-101 fixture, and conceptual demonstrator.
2. **Phase 2 — Interactive Twin Capsule:** edit and inspect bounded asset representations.
3. **Phase 3 — Synthetic Experiment Workbench:** configure controlled, explicitly synthetic investigations.
4. **Phase 4 — Real Dataset Import:** introduce governed, traceable dataset ingestion.
5. **Phase 5 — Python Experiment Runtime:** execute reproducible analytical workloads outside OT control.
6. **Phase 6 — MLflow / Experiment Registry:** preserve experiment and model lineage.
7. **Phase 7 — FMU / Modelica Simulation:** connect validated simulation artifacts through explicit interfaces.
8. **Phase 8 — Local LLM AI Scientist:** orchestrate tools and evidence locally without control authority.
9. **Phase 9 — Industrial Connectors:** add controlled industrial data access under a separate security design.
10. **Phase 10 — Fleet Intelligence:** test portability and organizational learning across assets.

The roadmap documents extension points; it is not hidden partial implementation or a promise that every phase is appropriate for every plant. Any control-facing capability requires its own hazard analysis, security boundary, validation case, approval process, and accountable human authority.
